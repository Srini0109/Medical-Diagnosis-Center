# Medical-Diagnosis-Center
Centralized Medical Diagnostic Center: a web platform for booking diagnostic tests, tracking samples, verifying results and delivering reports to patients in one place.
Centralized Medical Diagnostic Center

This project is a centralized platform that brings the whole diagnostic process into one system, from booking a test to receiving the final report.

Patients can register, browse tests and health check-up packages, book a slot, and track their sample status.

Technicians generate barcodes for samples, update sample status, and enter test results.

Doctors review and approve results before they are released to patients.

Patients get notified when a report is ready, can download it as a PDF, and can view their past reports in a single health record.

Admins manage roles and access, monitor bookings and turnaround time on a dashboard, and review audit logs.
